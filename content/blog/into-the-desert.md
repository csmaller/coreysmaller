Sitting in the Battle Van boondocking in Moab, writing my thoughts.  I had to get away for a while. Had a gig this saturday and cancelled it. I can't stand to be around people let alone be their entertainment.  I have no desire to play my guitar or continue learning the harmonica.  My new amp sits there, unplayed.  I try and motivate.  Just now I pulled out the guitar, played "Loser" (the Dead version, not the Beck version) and some blues riffs, but the vibe aint there.  I put it back in it's case.

 I want to sulk. I want to cry but I don't think I am capable anymore.  I am numb, desensitized.I don't want any of this right now: This country, this relationship, this job, this life. Sick of it all

  I thought getting away would do it. Getting my head right in the desert. That sort of Jesus / Jim Morrison romantic notion that all will be well after a trip to the dez.   

But it's not, and here we are.  

I hope I snap out of it. 

I hope the world snaps out of it.  

I hope people start to see how important kindness is.
