The Four Agreements with additions for a long, happy life. 



1. Always come from a place of Love
2. Be impeccable with your words
3. Don't make assumptions
4. Always do your best
5. Only put good food into your body
6. Move your body every day
7. Laugh loudly and often
8. Travel 
9. Express yourself creatively and uniquely
10. Always keep learning
11. Listen 
12. Keep an open mind
